Frequently Asked Questions
How do I make a deposit? ▼
Go to Transfer in the sidebar. Fill in the recipient details, choose your source account, enter the amount and confirm. Funds arrive within 1–3 business days.
How long do transfers take? ▼
Standard ACH transfers take 1–3 business days. Same Day ACH is available for a $2.50 fee. Wire transfers typically complete within 24 hours for a $25 fee.
Is my money FDIC insured? ▼
Yes. Synchrony Bank is FDIC-insured (Certificate #27314). Deposits are insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category.
How is APY calculated? ▼
APY reflects your actual return with daily compounding, credited monthly. At 3.30% APY, a $10,000 deposit earns ~$330 per year.
How do I apply for a loan? ▼
Click Loans in the sidebar, pick your loan type, and click "Apply Now." Most applications take under 5 minutes and start with a soft credit check.
How do I change my password? ▼
Go to Settings → Security → Change Password. A reset link will be sent to your registered email. The link expires in 30 minutes.
