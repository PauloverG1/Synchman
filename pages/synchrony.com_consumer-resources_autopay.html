Autopay

Simplify your payments with autopay. Synchrony autopay takes the hassle out of managing your bills. Once you set it up, payments are automatically made on time, every time.

Sign up today and take one more task off your to-do list

Convenience

Set it once and let us do the rest.

Always on time

No more missed payments or late fees.

Peace of mind

Relax knowing your payments are always on track.

How to get started

It’s quick and easy. Just follow these steps to activate autopay in minutes.

1

Sign in to the online account for your card

2

Select the Autopay option to access settings

3

Choose payment amount and date

4

Add or confirm payment method

FAQs

Have questions? We have answers.

With autopay, your payment will be automatically deducted from your bank account each month on your payment due date. The payment will be the amount you select: either the statement balance, the total minimum payment due or a self-selected (other) amount.
No, the deductions will be suspended when your account has a zero balance. Autopay will resume when your account has a balance.
  1. From the home page, select the Autopay-Off link located below the Make Payment button.
  2. Click the Enroll in autopay button
  3. Select the payment amount and payment source
  4. Agree to the terms
  5. Confirm autopay
  6. Review your autopay settings
  7. Click Submit
 

You will see a confirmation screen showing your autopay settings and a confirmation number. We'll also send you a confirmation email to the email address on file for your account.

There is no fee to enroll or use autopay. Your card’s annualized percentage rate (APR) will still apply to any unpaid balance each month.
