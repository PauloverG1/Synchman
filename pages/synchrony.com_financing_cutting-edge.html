FAQ

Have questions? We have answers.

What are the Cutting Edge credit card benefits?
  • No annual fee1
  • Safe and Secure with $0 fraud liability
  • Ability to request a credit limit increase3
  • Convenient monthly payments
  • 24/7 secure account access
  • Customizable account alerts
How do I change my personal information (email address, address, etc.)?

To change your address, email address, phone number, or personal information on your account, please visit www.synchrony.com.

What if I have account management questions?

Cutting Edge credit cardholders can manage their account online by registering / logging in to chat with us (chat is unavailable in Internet Explorer) if you need additional account assistance. You may also call 1-800-250-5411 to speak to a customer service representative.

Can I schedule multiple payments?

You can schedule up to 12 future payments, including the date you want the payment to post to your account and the amount. You can also vary the amount, date and payment source for each payment.
