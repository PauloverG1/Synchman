How to register your credit card account

It takes just a few minutes to register and set up your payment method. Let’s get started!

Register today Download the PDF

Note: To register a retail card, please navigate to your retail card’s login page. All other cardholders can register by following the steps below.

Get more value from your credit card issued by Synchrony

Enjoy all the advantages of registering your account online! We know you’re busy working forward to all the important things in life. That’s why we want to make sure you have a fast, convenient and secure way to manage many of your credit card accounts issued by Synchrony. By registering at synchrony.com, you can easily track account activity, schedule payments, view statements and more — anytime, anywhere, from your computer or mobile device.

Here's how to get started!

Step 1: Gather your information

Please have the following items handy when you’re ready to register your Synchrony credit card account and set up payment methods:

  • Your Synchrony account number
  • Last four digits of your Social Security number
  • Your date of birth

Step 2: Look up your account

  • Go to the synchrony.com Registration page.
  • Or, in the MySynchrony Mobile App, click Register on the log in page.
  • Enter your account number, the last four digits of your Social Security number and your date of birth, and then click Continue.
  • When you begin entering information into each field, you’ll see quick tip boxes appear on your screen.

Step 3: Verify your account information

  • Review and verify that the account information shown is correct, and then click Continue.
  • If the information is not correct, please call the phone number shown on the screen for assistance.

Step 4: Check multi-account status

  • If you have more than one credit card issued by Synchrony, you will have the opportunity to include additional accounts in this one-time registration.
  • When you are done, click Continue.

Step 5A: Set up your profile

  • Create a user name and password.

Step 5B: Set up your profile

  • Create three security questions and answers.
  • Enter your email address.
  • You will have the option to request paperless statements.
  • Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
  • Click Continue.

Step 6: Receive confirmation

  • When you’ve successfully registered, you’ll receive a confirmation message.
  • Click Go to My Account.

Gain the advantages of registration today!

Get ready to enjoy the convenience of online account management and so much more.

Questions?

Please call the phone number on the back of your Synchrony credit card if you have questions or need further assistance in registering your account.
