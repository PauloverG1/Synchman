Your credit score can have a major impact on loan approvals, interest rates and more. Find out your score and how to improve it when you enroll in Synchrony's free credit score program with VantageScore®VantageScore® is a top credit scoring model used by many lenders when they consider whether or not to approve applications and deide what rates and terms to offer. .
