Discover the advantages of Paperless Statements

Enjoy fast, secure, 24/7 digital account management with paperless statements when you register and sign in to your Synchrony credit card account online.

How to get started in 3 easy steps

1

Register your Synchrony credit card online1

Go to synchrony.com or open the MySynchrony mobile app 2 to register.

2

Add payment methods

When you enter payment methods, you can make same-day payments, schedule up to 12 payments in advance or sign up for automatic monthly payments.

3

Enroll in paperless statements

Go digital and update your statement delivery method from mail to paperless statements.

Set up Paperless Statements today. Find your account, or check the back of your credit card to get started!
